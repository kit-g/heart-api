# A coach's assistant

A coach can ask their own assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, anything connected to Heart's MCP server) about
the people they coach: who needs a word, what a client did last week, how far into a program they
are. The first version of coaching left the machine surfaces out (`2026-11-01.coaching.md`); this is
the read side, decided on 2026-11-09.

## What holds

- **The client decides.** A student opens what their coach reads to the coach's assistant with a
  share of its own, `assistant`, beside `history` and `goals` (`PUT /connections/:id/shares`). It is
  off until they turn it on. Sharing with a person isn't sharing with their software.
- **The assistant reads what the coach reads, never more.** Each read is the app's own coach read,
  narrowed in the statement that reads: `_relation(reader, owner, assistant)` answers nothing unless
  the share is on. History before coaching began still needs `history`, goals still need `goals`,
  and nothing hidden or from a pause shows. A pause shares nothing with the assistant at all, as it
  stops the other shares. A program copy is read in one snapshot with the check that allows it.
- **Peers are out.** A friend's view of you never reaches their assistant, whatever you share.
- **Its own scope, `read:coaching`.** A connection made before the scope existed doesn't start
  reading other people's training: calling a coach's tool on one is the `insufficient_scope` step-up,
  and the user reconnects. A new connection that asks for no scope gets it with the rest, and the
  consent page says so. Personal tokens never hold it.
- **Only a coach is offered the tools.** `tools/list` and the instructions name them to a connection
  whose user has had a client, former ones included, or has a library to send from. Nobody else's
  assistant carries them.
- **A client's words are quoted.** Everything that lives in the client's account (workout names and
  notes, exercise notes, their own exercises, a program and its sessions, a program's history) comes
  back under `quoted` with their name, so the model reads it as theirs and never as instructions.
  That includes what the coach sent or logged: the client can change it, and nothing records whose
  words are left after they do. `sentByYou` and `loggedByYou` say where it came from. Words another
  account wrote (another coach's session or exercise) are quoted as that account's. A workout is the
  exception: who logged one is its owner's and that coach's to know (heart-api#405), so another
  coach's logged workout reads as the client's.
- **The user's own id is no client.** A client tool asked about the user themselves is a tool error
  that points at the user's own tools, so `read:coaching` alone never reads the user's own log.
- **Messages and notes stay sealed.** The server can't open them, so no tool can return them.
- **Read-only.** Nothing here sends, edits, logs or messages.

## Tools

All take the `read:coaching` scope. A client is addressed by the `clientId` `list_clients` returns.

| Tool                         | Reads                                                                    |
|------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `list_clients`               | the roster: everyone's name, status and since, and `sharesWithAssistant`; for those who share, their program, recent training and reasons |
| `get_coach_feed`             | the feed, across those who share                                         |
| `list_client_workouts`       | one client's workouts, summarized                                        |
| `get_client_workout`         | one workout in full, targets beside sets                                 |
| `list_client_programs`       | the copies the coach sent them                                           |
| `get_client_program`         | one copy: sessions, what's done, where they stand                        |
| `get_client_program_changes` | who changed what in that copy                                            |
| `list_client_goals`          | their goals, when they share goals too                                   |
| `list_my_library`            | the coach's own library                                                  |
| `get_my_library_program`     | one library program, its current version, exercises by name             |

A client who doesn't share is listed by name and status alone, `sharesWithAssistant: false`. A client
tool asked about them is a tool error that points at `list_clients`. The coach's app learns who shares
from the same field on the roster (`ClientSummary.assistant`).

## Gaps

- **`/me` has no coaching routes yet.** They wait on personal tokens being able to hold
  `read:coaching`, which takes a choice when a token is made, in the app. An OAuth grant to `/me`
  would be their only reader until then (heart-api#412).
- **No per-client numbers.** Records, volume and exercise history for a client are a next round:
  they're new queries, not reuse.
- **The ChatGPT plugin's listing** says nothing about coaching yet. Its next submission should, with a
  review case for a coach.

## Compatibility

Additive. A new scope, a new share value, a new roster field and new tools. A host connected before
this sees no change until the user reconnects. The consent page that names the scope ships with the
site, so the site deploys first: a coach connecting in between would be granted the scope by a page
that doesn't mention it. An app build that doesn't know the share carries it as
it arrived, and the server's roster field reads as false to it.
