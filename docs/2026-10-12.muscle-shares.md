# How much of a set each muscle receives

A bench press set counts fully for the chest and about half for the triceps.
Written from the code as it stands (heart-api#197). It feeds the app's sets per
muscle per week (`docs/2026-10-11.pattern-lens.md` in heart-of-yours).

## Why it exists

The volume research counts indirect work as a fraction of a set. A count that
sees only primary muscles undercounts triceps from every press and chest from
dips. The app shows whole numbers and keeps the fractions underneath.

## The rule, and where the exceptions live

A library exercise tags its muscles in two roles, `primary` and `secondary`.
Each role has a share of one set that every muscle in it receives:

- **The default** is 1 for primary and 0.5 for secondary. It is a constant in
  `heart_models` (`MuscleTagging.defaultPrimaryShare`,
  `defaultSecondaryShare`), not content, so every exercise has an answer
  without anyone writing one.
- **An exception** is content. A role in `content/exercise_library.yml` may
  carry `share`:

  ```yaml
  muscles:
    primary:
      ids: [biceps_brachii_l, biceps_brachii_r]
    secondary:
      ids: [brachioradialis_l, brachioradialis_r]
      share: 0.25
  ```

  The schema bounds it to (0, 1]. The sync carries it into the
  `exercises.muscles` blob, and the library (API and CDN) serves it beside the
  ids and groups. A role without one leaves the key out.

`MuscleTagging.shareOf(muscle)` gives the answer for a muscle id prefix
(`triceps_brachii`) or a group (`chest`), matched the way library search
matches muscles. Primary wins when a muscle sits in both roles. A muscle the
exercise doesn't tag gets 0.

Older builds ignore the key, and nothing they write carries library muscles.

## Gaps

- No exceptions are written yet: every exercise uses the default. Curating
  them is content work, one role at a time, where the default is clearly
  wrong.
- A share applies to the whole role. Two secondary muscles that need
  different shares can't be expressed without a per-muscle map. None needs
  one yet.
- User-created exercises tag no muscles, so they count toward no muscle. The
  pattern lens asks for a pattern on creation; muscles are the same open
  question.
