In [What Heart is](what-heart-is.html) I said the Watch app only existed on iOS. Not anymore. This is the blog catch-up for 1.16, released on October 25.

**Heart on Wear OS.** Log sets from your wrist on a Wear OS watch, with rest and last time's numbers. Open Heart on the watch to start.

The rest of this release is the stuff you do in your head between sets, done for you.

**Plate calculator.** Type a barbell or machine weight and see what goes on each side. Set the bar per exercise and untick the plates your gym is missing.

**Warm-up sets.** Worked out from your first working set and rounded to what you can actually load. Add them from an exercise's menu. The ramp is yours to change.

**Default rest.** A rest timer after every set, even for exercises you never gave one: one length for big compound lifts, one for isolation, one for bodyweight and cardio. Your own timers still win.

**Add a session to a program.** Add a template to a program you're already running, from the program's page or from the template's menu in its folder, and your weeks and starting weights stay where they are.

And a couple of things for looking at your training:

- a muscle map for templates, while you plan them and before you start
- new charts: volume, working sets and average workout length by week or month, and a heatmap of the days you trained a lift

The plate calculator, warm-ups and default rest each ask once before they show up. Say no, or turn one off in Settings › Features later, and it's like it was never built.
