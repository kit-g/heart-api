This release has a first in it: feedback from people who've been lifting for years and opened Heart with fresh eyes. Some of what they said is already in.

**Log a past workout.** What if the workout already happened? Say when it was, then what you did, and it goes straight to History. No clock running, no rest timers going off for a session that ended yesterday. It's a switch in Settings › Features, because most people log live and don't need another button.

**Create an exercise from the picker.** Building a template and the exercise isn't in the library? You used to have to back out, create it somewhere else and come back. Now you can create it right there, or during a workout. Your own exercises can be tagged with the muscles they work too.

**Some things I'd made core are now switches.** Sets a week and the example templates stay on, but if you know what you're doing and they're noise, Settings › Features turns them off and the screens close up around the gap.

Also new, and each of these asks before it shows up:

**Recaps.** Early each month, last month on your profile: workouts, time, sets, volume, the muscles and patterns you trained, the records you set. Every month stays in a list, and you can share a report as a card.

**Copy for AI.** A workout, a month or one exercise's history as text that any assistant reads well. Paste it into whatever you use and ask it something. Health data stays out of the copy.

**Live records.** A quiet note the moment you tick a set, if it beats your best.

**Second look.** Left the timer running for hours? Heart points out entries that look like a slip and you decide: keep it, or leave it out of your records and charts. Needs an account.

And on the watch: with the set stopwatch on, you can start and stop a timed hold from your Apple Watch or Wear OS watch now, and the time lands on your phone.

If you've been lifting a while and something in Heart gets in your way, say so [on r/heartofyours](https://www.reddit.com/r/heartofyours/). That's how this release happened.
