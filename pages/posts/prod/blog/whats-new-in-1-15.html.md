In [What Heart is](what-heart-is.html) I promised Programs would get a post of its own. This is the blog catch-up for 1.15, originally released in October.

Up to 1.15 Heart was a log: it remembered what you did. Now it can also nudge you a little in a direction you choose to go.

**Programs.** 19 ready-made ones, each with the research behind it, sources included, so you can go read why a program works the way it does. Or take a folder of your own templates and make it a program. Heart puts the sessions on the days you pick, fills in every set's target and adds weight when you've earned it: a training max for wave programs, a working weight for the classics, harder steps for bodyweight ones. Starting weights come from your own history, and you can override any of them.

**Not today.** Skip a planned session in one tap, name a reason if you feel like it. The program waits, no guilt, promise! Life happens.

**Things to go easy on.** A knee, heavy weight on the spine, an old wound (sheesh, do I have those myself). Heart marks the exercises that are harder on it and suggests kinder swaps. The list you keep for yourself stays on this device, under my "no health data" promise. A program can carry go-easy picks of its own, and those travel with the program.

And a bunch of smaller things:

- program sessions in your phone's calendar, Google's included
- supersets and circuits
- template sets can aim at a rep range, an exact count, a weight or reps in reserve
- sets per week by movement pattern or muscle, and how push vs pull and hinge vs squat balance out
- goals on a pattern ("presses per week") or a muscle, not just one lift
- a record badge on the set that earned it
- pick the day your week starts
- in a workout from a template, Previous can show the last time you ran that template

Don't need Programs? Settings › Features, and it's gone like it was never there.
