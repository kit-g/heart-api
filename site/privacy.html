Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Last reviewed against the app’s behaviour: September 5, 2026.

Thanks for using Heart of Yours. This document explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

We believe your workout data is your own. We store it carefully, don’t share it, and don’t lock it behind a paywall.

Your health data is a different matter: we don’t store it at all. Anything Heart reads from Apple Health or Health Connect stays on your phone. More on that below.

If anything here feels off, you're welcome to stop using the app — but we hope you won’t have to.

What We Collect

Account Information

To create an account, you’ll need to provide an email address. That’s it. Names are optional.

You can sign in using your email, or a supported third-party login (like Sign in with Apple). If you lose access to that method, we won’t be able to recover your account.

Workout Data

This is the core of the app: your sets, reps, weights, and sessions. It’s stored securely in the cloud and linked to your account. You can always view, update, or delete your data.

Health Data (Apple Health and Health Connect)

If you choose to, Heart can read a few health metrics from your phone’s health store — Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android — and show them next to your training. This only happens after you grant permission in the operating system’s own sheet, and you can decline or revoke it at any time.

What Heart reads: resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, steps, active energy, and body mass. Nothing else. Each reading also carries which app or device recorded it, so Heart can tell a watch from a phone and avoid double-counting.

Where it goes: nowhere. Heart keeps a copy on your phone so charts work offline, and that copy never leaves the device. It is not uploaded to our servers, not included in analytics, and not shared with anyone. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or anything other than showing it back to you. Crash reports are stripped of anything that could contain a health value, and if you send us a bug report with a screenshot, health numbers are blacked out before the picture is taken.

What Heart writes: with your separate permission, Heart can save each workout you finish to your health store, so your activity rings credit your lifting. It writes when you started, when you finished, and what kind of workout it was — nothing else. Heart does not estimate calories or heart rate, and it never writes anything you didn’t do. Settings → Health shows whether it’s on and takes you to your phone’s health permissions to change it.

Your controls:

  • Change or revoke what Heart may read in the Health app (iPhone: your profile → Apps) or in Health Connect (Android).
  • Delete Heart’s copy from your phone under Settings → Health → Delete health data. Your phone’s health store is untouched; Heart will read it again the next time it syncs, if you still allow it.
  • Deleting the app removes Heart’s copy entirely.

Because health data is never on our servers, deleting your Heart account doesn’t affect it either way: the copy on your phone stays until you delete it or the app, and your phone’s health store keeps everything it had. On Android, Heart’s local database is excluded from device backups. On iPhone it is part of the app’s data, so it is included in your device backup if you have one — that backup is yours, and we never see it.

App Usage & Debug Info

We collect basic analytics through Firebase Analytics (like which screens are used) and crash logs to help improve the app. This data is anonymous, never sold, and never includes your health data.

Your Choices

Exporting Data

Data export features are in development. In the meantime, you can request a copy of your data by emailing info@heart-of.me.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your Heart of Yours account — and all your data — from inside the app. To do that:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings (tap on your username or the Gear icon)
  2. Tap Account Control
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm deletion

If you signed in with Google or Apple, you’ll first need to set a password. This ensures you can securely confirm the deletion.

Once confirmed, your account and all synced workout data will be permanently removed. Deleted means deleted. Health data was never there to begin with — see above for how to clear the copy on your phone.

Other Details

Storage

We store user data on secure servers operated by AWS. That may include servers located outside your country.

Diagnostics and Crash Reporting

To help improve the app, we use Sentry to collect crash reports and error diagnostics. These reports may include technical details like device type, OS version, and logs related to the crash — but they do not include your workout data, your health data, or personal identifiers. Errors in the health feature are reported with their message removed, so a value can’t slip through inside one. We use this information solely to fix bugs and improve stability.

Children

This app isn’t designed for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us.

Policy Changes

This policy may evolve. If anything important changes, we’ll let you know.

Simple as that. If you have questions, email info@heart-of.me.
