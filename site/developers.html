Developer API

Your training log, readable by your own scripts, spreadsheets and AI assistants. It’s free and read-only: it can show what you logged, and it can’t change anything.

There’s no health data in it, because Heart never has any. Heart rate, sleep, body weight and the rest stay on your phone, in Apple Health or Health Connect. A workout’s calories is an estimate from the exercises and the weight you entered, never a watch reading.

curl https://api.heart-of.me/v1/me/workouts?limit=5 \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer hrt_…" \
  -H "User-Agent: my-sheet-sync/1.0"

Tokens

Create a token in the app under Settings. (The screen for it comes with an upcoming app update.) You need a signed-in account: without one, your log lives only on your phone and there’s nothing on the server to read. You see a token’s secret once, when you create it; Heart stores only a digest of it. You can hold five active tokens, each living a year or until you revoke it.

Send it as a bearer token. A missing, unknown, revoked or expired token gets the same answer:

401 {"error": "unauthorized", "code": "invalid_token", "reason": "…"}

Please set a User-Agent that names your tool. It isn’t required, but it helps us see what people build.

Limits

20 requests a minute and 200 a day per account, shared by all of its tokens. That covers a few questions, a nightly sync and a one-off history pull with room to spare. Past either limit:

429 {"error": "too many requests", "code": "rate_limited", "reason": "…", "retryAfter": 41}

with a Retry-After header in seconds.

What you can read

Everything is GET, and everything is about the account the token belongs to. Lists that page carry a cursor while there’s more: pass it back as ?cursor=. No cursor means the last page. limit is 1–100 (30 by default).

RouteReturns
/meYour username, unit system, and how many workouts, templates, folders, goals and custom exercises you have.
/me/workoutsYour workouts, newest first, each with its exercises, sets, notes and image links.
/me/workouts/:idOne workout.
/me/workouts/changesWhat changed since a cursor: see Keeping a copy in sync.
/me/recordsPersonal records per exercise, the same ones the app shows; ?exerciseId= for one.
/me/exercises/:id/historyEvery session of one exercise, newest first, with its working sets and the values the app’s progress chart plots.
/me/library?q=The exercise library and your own exercises, searched the way the app searches: word order free, abbreviations such as db or rdl, muscle words such as lats, one typo a word.
/me/exercisesYour custom exercises.
/me/templatesYour templates, in your order; ?folder= for one folder.
/me/template-foldersYour template folders.
/me/goalsYour goals. Goals backed by health data carry their definition only; their progress lives on your phone.

Weights are in kilograms and distances in kilometres, whatever unit the app shows you. Durations are in seconds, and times are ISO 8601 in UTC.

Keeping a copy in sync

To keep a spreadsheet or a home dashboard current, poll /me/workouts/changes instead of re-reading everything. Without since it starts from the beginning; after that, pass back the cursor you were given. Each change is a whole workout as it stands now, or the id of one that was deleted. While hasMore is true, ask again straight away. When nothing changed you get empty lists and the same cursor, so keep it.

Every few minutes is plenty: polling faster gains nothing and uses up the daily limit. If a poll answers 400 with stale_cursor, start again without since.

Export

/me/export?format=strong downloads your whole history as a CSV in Strong’s export format, which most workout apps can import, Heart included. Weights and distances are in your unit. One export a day.

curl -L "https://api.heart-of.me/v1/me/export?format=strong" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer hrt_…" -o heart.csv

You don’t need the API for this: the app exports too, under Settings → Export my data.

AI assistants (MCP)

The same token connects an AI assistant to your log over MCP, read-only. In Claude Code:

claude mcp add --transport http heart https://api.heart-of.me/v1/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer hrt_…"

In Cursor, Claude Desktop or any client with a JSON config:

{"mcpServers": {"heart": {
  "url": "https://api.heart-of.me/v1/mcp",
  "headers": {"Authorization": "Bearer hrt_…"}
}}}

The assistant can read your profile, workouts, templates and folders, goals, personal records, an exercise’s history, your custom exercises and the exercise library. It can’t change anything. Only the data it reads counts against your limits, not the tool listing it does at the start of a conversation. What it reads goes to the assistant’s provider and is covered by their privacy policy, not ours.

Compatibility

Fields and routes may be added; none will be renamed or removed. Ignore fields you don’t know.
