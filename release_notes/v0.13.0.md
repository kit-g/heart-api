## Developers

New on the token surface (`/me`, same limits as before) and on MCP. Detail:
https://heart-of.me/developers.html, and the design docs under
https://github.com/kit-g/heart-api/tree/main/docs.

- `GET /me/programs`, `GET /me/programs/:programId`: your programs, where you are in each, the
  current bases and stages, the next session planned, and the research behind a library program.
- `GET /me/programs/:programId/history`: how each base moved and why, the actions taken, the
  sessions skipped. An unknown id is `404 program_not_found`.
- `GET /me/library/programs`, `GET /me/library/programs/:key`: Heart's program library. An
  unknown key is `404 library_program_not_found`.
- `GET /me/volume?from=&to=`: weekly sets per muscle and movement pattern, duration and volume.
- `GET /me/balance?from=&to=`: how often each pair of opposing muscles was trained.
- `GET /me/goals/progress`: each live goal with its current value and progress.
- Volume and balance cover the last 4 weeks by default, a year at most.
- `GET /me` counts `programs`; `GET /me/records` gives each record's `lastTrained`; goals carry
  `pattern` and `muscle`.
- MCP adds `get_training_volume`, `get_balance`, `get_goal_progress`, `list_programs`,
  `get_program`, `get_program_history`, `list_library_programs` and `get_library_program`.
  `get_personal_records` adds `lastTrained`, `list_custom_exercises` adds `pattern`. The server
  names its website and icon, and its instructions ask a model to treat entries as typed by hand:
  medians over means, outliers pointed out.
