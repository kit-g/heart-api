# v0.15.0: MCP cards in your units, rebuilt on Heart's design tokens

Previous tag: v0.14.0 (heart_models 2.39.0, unchanged; heart_aws 1.1.0, unchanged; heart_design
1.0.0, new, consumed by the API only).

## Compatibility

Every contract change since v0.14.0:

| Change | Kind |
|---|---|
| MCP: a tool whose result draws a card (`get_workout`, `get_exercise_history`, `get_personal_records`, `create_template`, `update_template`) adds `unitSystem` (`metric` or `imperial`, the units the user logs in) to its result, and a second text item saying how to treat the card | additive (MCP only) |

Nothing changed on the app's surface: no route, request, response, status code or error `code`,
no `heart_models` change, no migration. The MCP cards were rebuilt in Jaspr on the shared design
tokens; the pages they serve are internal to the cards.

Oldest app served correctly: **1.12**, as before. `MIN_APP_VERSION` stays **1.13.0**, not
enforced.

## Developers

MCP only; the token surface (`/me`) is unchanged. Detail: https://heart-of.me/developers.html.

- Cards draw in the user's units. The five card tools above return `unitSystem`; weights and
  distances in the data stay in kg and km, as before, and the card converts them. A client that
  doesn't draw cards gets the same data plus the new key.
- Beside each such result, a second text item tells the assistant that a host drawing Heart cards
  already shows the result, so it shouldn't restate it, and to give weights and distances in
  `unitSystem`.
- The cards themselves: the workout card collapses repeated sets and lays a long workout out in
  columns when the frame is wide; progress, records and template cards share its look.

## Before tagging

1. Nothing in Terraform, and no migrations.
2. After the deploy: in a connected ChatGPT or Claude, `get_workout` draws its card, and for an
   account set to imperial it shows lb and mi.
