# v0.14.1: MCP cards in the user's units

Previous tag: v0.14.0 (heart_models 2.39.0 and heart_aws 1.1.0, unchanged).

## Compatibility

Every contract change since v0.14.0:

| Change | Kind |
|---|---|
| MCP: `get_workout`, `get_exercise_history`, `get_personal_records`, `create_template` and `update_template` add `unitSystem` to their results, and a second text item to their `content` (heart-api#320) | additive |

Nothing an app build sends or reads changed. Oldest app served correctly: **1.12**, as before.
`MIN_APP_VERSION` stays **1.13.0**, not enforced.

## Developers

- The MCP cards show weights and distances in the user's units: pounds and miles for an imperial
  user, pace per mile included. The tools still measure in kg and km.
- The tools that draw a card name the user's `unitSystem` in their results, and say beside them
  that a host showing the card has the model answer the question rather than restate it.
- The workout card puts repeated sets on one line, and lays exercises out in columns when the
  host's column is wide.
